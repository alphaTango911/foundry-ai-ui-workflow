# Usability test — QUICKSTART.md

**Goal:** find where someone else gets stuck reproducing the workflow without help. This is one observation, not evidence of adoption.

## Setup

- **Participant:** one designer or front-end developer who hasn't seen this repo.
- **Give them:** the repo link and one sentence: "Follow QUICKSTART.md as far as you can. Think out loud. I won't help unless you're completely stuck."
- **Time box:** 45 minutes.
- **You:** observe, take notes, don't explain. If you must intervene, note exactly when and what you said.

## Tasks

1. Get the reference panel to GO (QUICKSTART §2).
2. Run the eval set and say, in their own words, what case 10 means (§3).
3. Generate one panel with a tool of their choice and run the checker on it (§4, steps 1–2).
4. Optional: review and repair.

- Would you trust a GO from this? Why or why not?
